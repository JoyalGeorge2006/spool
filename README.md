# spool

A music player for Android.

</div>

## Status

spool is being rebuilt with a Figtree-based interface, light and dark themes, and the spool identity. This work is in progress. A finished APK will be shared after implementation and verification; older test builds are not the finished redesign.

## Music and playback

- Browse, search and play music through supported sources.
- Listen to local music and manage offline downloads.
- Adjust playback, per-network audio quality and output settings.
- Read synced lyrics when a provider has them.
- Use the existing account and service integrations where available.

Some features depend on source availability, account access, network conditions or device support. Source quality and availability are not guaranteed.

## Source and builds
